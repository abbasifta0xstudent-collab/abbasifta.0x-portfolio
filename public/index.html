Synopsis & Themes

"سفرِ زیاں" (The Journey of Loss and Redemption) is an introspective novel exploring existential challenges, personal loss, resilience against societal pressures, and the spiritual quest for truth. It narrates the moral voyage of a resilient soul through unpredictable tribulations.
